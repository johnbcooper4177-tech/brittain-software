SMS Program Description

The Brittain Software Hospitality SMS Program sends requested hospitality-related messages for supported restaurant and hotel workflows. Messages may include reservation confirmations or requests, booking or checkout links, property information, restaurant information, addresses, and support phone numbers related to a recipient's phone call or request.

SMS Consent

You may opt in to receive SMS messages by providing verbal consent during an inbound phone call or by another consent method presented by Brittain Software or its supported hospitality clients. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

When verbal opt-in is used, the caller is told what type of text will be sent, that message frequency varies, that message and data rates may apply, and that they can reply HELP for help. They can reply STOP to opt out. SMS messages are sent only after the caller gives affirmative consent.

The exact verbal consent script is available at Brittain Software Hospitality SMS Opt-In Script.

Message frequency varies based on the recipient's request and interaction with the service, usually 1-2 messages per reservation or information request. Message and data rates may apply.
