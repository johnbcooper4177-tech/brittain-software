Angie

Restaurant phone concierge

Angie handles the main phone line with brief, natural answers. She knows restaurant facts, menu details, current pricing, hours, policies, location, seating preferences, private dining basics, specials, and reservation rules.

  • Answers menu, pricing, hours, location, parking, dress code, and policy questions.
  • Searches useful reservation options instead of reading long availability grids.
  • Captures booking or follow-up details when staff review is needed.

Live workflow

Routine calls become booked or follow-up-ready outcomes.

Angie collects only the missing details, checks availability, gives a concise recap, and closes the loop with guest confirmation when the reservation path is allowed by business rules.

Call flows

Designed for high-volume restaurant phone traffic.

01

Knowledge-only calls

Angie answers questions about menu items, prices, specials, hours, directions, parking, dress code, allergies, seating, ocean views, private dining, delivery, and policies.

02

Simple reservations

Angie gathers date, time, party size, seating preference, and guest name, then uses the caller's number as the default callback or confirmation number.

03

Broad availability search

When a caller asks what is available tonight or within a time range, Angie summarizes a small set of useful options and books after the guest chooses.

04

Policy-aware follow-up

Large parties, special events, waitlist needs, changes, cancellations, and staff-review requests can become structured follow-up items instead of messy phone messages.

Operations layer

Knowledge, quality, and outcomes keep improving.

The restaurant system pairs live answering with post-call services that help operators review quality, understand reservation outcomes, refine website and menu facts, and spot patterns in guest demand.
